---
name: Addition variations
description: Meaning of trade additions to existing quoted work.
---

Use an Addition variation for extra materials or work on an existing quote item. Keep the original item and its price intact; submit only the additional amount for the existing client-approval process.

**Why:** The user wants a distinct type for adding something to an existing line item, such as extra material, rather than calling it a substitution or site issue.

**How to apply:** Preserve links to the existing work in create/edit/reload and show the relationship to both trade and client. Do not duplicate the original item or silently fold the extra price into its base amount.
